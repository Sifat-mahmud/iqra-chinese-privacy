Privacy Policy

Iqra Chinese (package: com.iqrachinese) · Effective date: October 6, 2026

Iqra Chinese ("the app", "we", "us") is an Android app for learning HSK Chinese vocabulary, developed by Sifat Mahmud. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: when you sign in, we use Firebase Authentication, which processes your authentication identifier (such as an email address or account ID) and sign-in credentials.
  • Learning progress: words mastered, test attempts and results, XP, streaks, achievements, daily study goal, and study time. This is stored on your device and may be synced to your account.
  • App settings: preferences such as showing pinyin or meanings, your daily goal, and reminder times.
  • Groups (if you use them): your display name and progress information that is visible to other members of a group you join.
  • Technical information: basic device and app diagnostic data (for example crash reports) that helps us fix bugs, where enabled.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or payment information.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the app's features: sign-in, saving and restoring your progress, tests, statistics, and groups.
  • To send study reminders and alarms that you configure on your device.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, and fix errors.
  • To improve the app and its content.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not show third-party advertising in the app.

3. Data stored on your device

Vocabulary, sentences, audio, and much of your progress are stored locally on your device, and the app's core learning features work offline. Reminders are scheduled locally using Android's notification and alarm features; reminder settings are not sent to us.

4. Sharing of information

We share data only with service providers that help us run the app, and only as needed:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and cloud database services) processes account and progress data on our behalf. See Firebase's privacy information.
  • Other group members can see your display name and progress if you choose to join a group.
  • Legal reasons: we may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by emailing i.sifatmahmud@gmail.com from the address linked to your account, or by using the account deletion option in the app where available. We will delete the data within 30 days, except where we must keep it for legal reasons. Uninstalling the app removes the data stored locally on your device.

6. Security

Data sent between the app and our services is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your choices

  • You can turn off notifications and reminders in the app or in Android settings.
  • You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data using the contact below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the effective date updated above.
