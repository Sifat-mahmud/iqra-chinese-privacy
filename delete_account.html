Delete Your Account and Data

App: Iqra Chinese · Developer: Sifat Mahmud

You can ask Sifat Mahmud to delete your Iqra Chinese account and the data associated with it at any time. Follow the steps below.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to iqrachineseapp@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Iqra Chinese account.
  2. In the email, include your account identifier: the email address you signed in with. If you never signed in, tell us and we will explain how to remove locally stored data (uninstalling the app removes all data stored on your device).
  3. We will confirm your request by reply and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What data is deleted

DataAction
Account identifier (user ID, email address, if any)Deleted
Profile and in-app settingsDeleted
Content and activity you created in the appDeleted
Device identifiers linked to your accountDeleted

What data is kept

None. When we delete your account, all of your records are removed from our database (Firebase). We do not keep any of your data after deletion, and there is no additional retention period.

Delete data without deleting your account

If you only want specific data removed, email us at the address above and tell us which data. We will remove it and confirm by reply.
